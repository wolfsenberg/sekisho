## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: warm sumi charcoal grounds (sidebar darkest, canvas, raised paper-dark panels), kinari off-white ink, 1px hairlines in three values, one shu-vermilion seal ink for approval acts: primary actions, the active route, focus, the brand seal, chart ink and activity density. Status colours retuned to the palette (moss, yamabuki, carmine-pink, ai-blue), always with icon and text. Native-app calm at the level of macOS/iOS: filled controls instead of outlined boxes, 8px controls, 12px cards and 16px dialogs, a translucent sticky toolbar, menus that grow from their trigger. Geist for the interface; IBM Plex Mono (the old-tech accent) for figures, IDs, keys, timestamps, status chips, column heads, field labels and sidebar group labels, always sentence case. Prose never goes mono.
